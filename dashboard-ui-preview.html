Methodology

View Definitions & Assumptions

Wait averages

Use recorded waits only. Missing or invalid waits are excluded rather than treated as zero.

Date filters

Start and end dates are inclusive. Visit dates remain date-only values to avoid timezone shifts.

Weather

Place and dates are sent to Open-Meteo for context. Weather is not presented as a cause of visit patterns.
